# Bill of Materials – Est(r)car (Pre-Approval)

> Note: Prices are estimates and may vary depending on availability.

## Core Electronics
- Arduino Uno or Nano – $20–35  
  Used as the main microcontroller to process RC signals and control the system.

- RC Transmitter + Receiver (2–4 channels) – $50–70  
  Provides remote control input for speed and steering.

- Motor Driver (TB6612FNG or equivalent) – $10–15  
  Used to safely control DC motors from the Arduino.

- DC Gear Motors (2x) – $25–40  
  Provide propulsion for the 2WD drivetrain.

## Power System
- 2S Li-ion or LiPo Battery (7.4V) – $20–30  
  Powers the motors and electronics.

- Battery Holder / Case – $5–10  

- Voltage Regulator / Buck Converter (5V) – $5–10  
  Provides stable power to the Arduino and sensors.

- Power Switch – $3–5  

## Sensors
- Battery Voltage Sensor or Voltage Divider – $5–10  
  Allows monitoring battery level for safety.

- IMU (MPU6050) – $5–10 (optional)  
  Used to monitor basic movement and orientation.

## Mechanical Components
- Chassis (custom, kit, or fabricated) – $20–40  
- Wheels (2x) – $10–20  
- Caster Wheel – $5–10  
- Motor Mounts – $5–10  

## Wiring & Assembly
- Jumper Wires and Cables – $10–15  
- Connectors (JST / Dupont) – $5–10  
- Screws, Standoffs, Zip Ties – $10–15  

## Miscellaneous / Contingency
- Spare parts and replacements – $30–50  

## Estimated Total Budget
**$230 – $350 USD**

This budget is within the Hack Club Blueprint limit and allows flexibility for iteration and learning.
