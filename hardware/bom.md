# Bill of Materials – Est(r)car

## Core Electronics
- Arduino Uno or Nano
- RC Transmitter + Receiver (2+ channels)
- Motor Driver (TB6612FNG or L298N)
- DC Gear Motors (2x)
- Wheels (2x)
- Caster wheel (1x)

## Power
- Li-ion or LiPo battery (7.4V recommended)
- Battery holder or case
- Voltage regulator (5V)
- Power switch

## Sensors
- Battery voltage sensor (voltage divider or module)
- IMU (MPU6050) – optional

## Mechanical
- Chassis (custom or off-the-shelf)
- Motor mounts
- Screws, standoffs, zip ties

## Wiring
- Jumper wires
- Connectors (JST / Dupont)

## Estimated Budget
- Target: $250–350 USD
